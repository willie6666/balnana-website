---
title: Import from Settle Up
description: Move a group from Settle Up to Balnana with its expenses, payments and balances.
---

Coming from Settle Up? Your group can come along with its history.

1. In Settle Up, export the group as **CSV**.
2. In Balnana, choose **New group → Import** and pick the file.

Expenses, payments and members come along, and balances stay the same.

## Refunds and income

Imports keep refunds and income: spending totals subtract refunds and leave out income, while balances still include every transaction.

:::note
Groups imported before Balnana stored each transaction's direction show incorrect spending statistics. To fix them, import the original CSV again into a new group; an upgrade cannot work out the missing direction from the old balance rows.
:::

Balnana can also export any group to CSV from the app.
